Garden design guides

Start with the problem your garden needs to solve.

Good landscape ideas begin with the way a space is used: how people move through it, where light falls, how much maintenance is realistic, and what should feel different when the work is done. These guides turn common garden questions into practical prompts and AI-generated visual directions.

Before choosing a style

Record the facts that an image cannot know.

01

Measure the usable area

Note boundaries, doors, gates, steps, utilities, drainage points, and anything that must remain accessible.

02

Watch light and water

Record full sun, shade, wind, wet spots, and dry areas over more than one part of the day.

03

Define the primary use

Prioritize dining, play, planting, pets, entertaining, or quiet time instead of asking one small space to do everything.

04

Set a maintenance limit

Be honest about watering, pruning, cleaning, storage, and seasonal work before selecting materials or plants.

How to use AI well

Compare directions, then verify the details.

An AI garden image is useful when it helps you name what you want: a wider path, a more private seat, denser planting, or a calmer material palette. It cannot verify dimensions, utilities, plant suitability, drainage, structure, permits, or cost. Keep the parts that clarify your brief, then take the concept and your site notes to an appropriate local professional.
